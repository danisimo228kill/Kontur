The PMTiles files in data/regions contain Protomaps Basemap vector layers
based on OpenStreetMap and Natural Earth, as recorded in their metadata.
https://protomaps.com/
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/

The bundled maps are local extracts with limited geographic coverage and
zoom levels. Detailed region extracts and the Russia overview have different
levels of detail. The map data is not a live feed.
